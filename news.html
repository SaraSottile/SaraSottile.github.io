New paper in PLOS Computational Biology

Our paper, “Methotrexate’s effect on cells and adalimumab immunogenicity in axial spondyloarthritis: A mathematical study,” was published in PLOS Computational Biology.

The study develops a mathematical model to investigate how methotrexate may affect the immune response to adalimumab in axial spondyloarthritis.
