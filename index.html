Sara Sottile

Department of Medical and Surgical Sciences

Alma Mater Studiorum — University of Bologna

I work on mathematical modelling and analysis in epidemiology and biology, with a focus on dynamical systems, multiple time-scale dynamics, and Geometric Singular Perturbation Theory (GSPT). My work also includes Health Technology Assessment (HTA), cost-effectiveness analyses of healthcare interventions, simulation models, and statistical analysis and forecasting in COVID-19 and cancer epidemiology.
