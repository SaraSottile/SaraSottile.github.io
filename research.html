Research areas & projects

Research

My research combines mathematical modeling, medical statistics and epidemiology to study health-related questions, from theoretical epidemic dynamics to clinical and population data. The projects below highlight selected research themes, collaborations and related scientific outputs.

01 / THEORY

Mathematical Modeling & Dynamical Systems

Mathematical analysis of dynamical systems, with a focus on stability, multigroup models, multiple time scales and Geometric Singular Perturbation Theory (GSPT).

Epidemic Models and Dynamical Systems

Analysis of compartmental epidemic models, including multigroup systems, stability properties and the mathematical behavior of models with different time scales.

Collaboration
University of Trento and academic research collaborators
Research output
  • A geometric analysis of the SIRS model with secondary infections (2024)
  • Global stability of multi-group SAIRS epidemic models (2023)
  • A survey on Lyapunov functions for epidemic compartmental models (2023)
  • Global stability of SAIRS epidemic models (2022)
02 / EPIDEMICS

Epidemic Modeling, Information & Behavior

Epidemic dynamics on networks and graphs, the spread of information and misinformation, and the role of behavioral processes in disease transmission.

Information and Misinformation in Epidemic Models

Mathematical modeling of epidemic processes coupled with the spread of information and misinformation, using dynamical-systems methods to investigate how fast information dynamics affect epidemic behavior.

Collaboration
University of Trento and mathematical modeling collaborators
Research output
  • A geometric analysis of the SIRS compartmental model with fast information and misinformation spreading (2024)
  • A geometric analysis of the impact of large but finite switching rates on vaccination evolutionary games (2024)

Epidemic Transmission on Networks

Study of epidemic dynamics in heterogeneous and scale-free networks, including the role of network structure and time-varying transmission.

Collaboration
Academic research collaborations
Research output
  • A minimal model for multigroup adaptive SIS epidemics (2025)
  • How network properties and epidemic parameters influence stochastic SIR dynamics on scale-free random networks (2022)
  • Time-varying epidemic transmission in heterogeneous networks and applications to measles (2020)
03 / STATISTICS

Medical Statistics & Epidemiology

Statistical analysis of clinical, cohort and population-level data, including cancer and cardiovascular epidemiology, survival and longitudinal analysis, risk prediction, and data harmonization in international studies.

International Cohort Studies: ACC, INHANCE and IESC3

Work with international pooled studies and consortia, including data management, quality control, harmonization and statistical analysis. ACC work included research on coffee and tea consumption in relation to pancreatic cancer mortality. Within INHANCE, my work also includes research on occupational exposures and head and neck cancer.

Collaboration
Asia Cohort Consortium (ACC); International Head and Neck Cancer Epidemiology Consortium (INHANCE); International Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma Consortium (IESC3)
Research output
INHANCE occupational exposure research: in preparation. A related IESC3 research presentation is the talk Smoking and Alcohol Consumption and Risk of Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma: A Pooled Analysis of the IESC3 Consortium .

Global Trends in Colorectal Cancer (CRC)

Research focused on global trends in colorectal cancer, within the broader field of cancer epidemiology and statistical analysis of population data.

Collaboration
Cancer epidemiology research collaboration
Research output
Poster presented at the EACR–The Mark Foundation Joint Conference: Global Incidence of Early-Onset Colorectal Cancer: A WHO-Based Analysis from the Mid-20th Century to Present.

Cervical Cancer Biopsy Decision Support

Statistical and machine-learning methods, including principal component analysis, clustering and random forests, applied to support the analysis of cervical cancer biopsy data.

Collaboration
University of Bologna and AIRC
Research output
Article: in preparation.

FItPreV — Personalized Cardiovascular Prevention

Statistical and epidemiological analysis of a randomized pilot trial evaluating personalized cardiovascular prevention and its potential implementation in the Italian National Health Service.

Collaboration
Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore
Research output
Genetic Risk Scores and Wearables for CVD Prevention: Insights from the FItPreV Trial — poster presented at EUPHA 2025. Related publication: in preparation.

ORCHESTRA — COVID-19 Vaccine Response

Statistical analysis of immune response and antibody kinetics following SARS-CoV-2 vaccination in healthcare workers.

Collaboration
ORCHESTRA project and international co-authors
Research output
Antibody Kinetics of Immunological Memory in SARS-CoV-2-Vaccinated Healthcare Workers—The ORCHESTRA Project (2025)

Return to Work After Liver Transplantation

Within project BRIC-2022-ID25, statistical analysis of a patient dataset combining clinical information and interview data, in an interdisciplinary project investigating unemployment risk factors and return to work among liver transplant recipients.

Collaboration
Interdisciplinary project on occupational reintegration after liver transplantation
Research output
  • Reintegration into working life for liver transplant recipients: cross-disciplinary evaluation of risk factors for unemployment
  • Workforce reintegration for liver transplant recipients: interdisciplinary assessment of unemployment risk factors
04 / HEALTH ECONOMICS

Health Technology Assessment

Health technology assessment and economic evaluation of healthcare interventions, particularly cost-effectiveness analyses of influenza vaccination strategies in different populations and healthcare settings.

Influenza Vaccination: Health Technology Assessment and Economic Evaluation

Health technology assessment and cost-effectiveness analyses of influenza vaccination strategies across different populations and healthcare settings, including pediatric vaccination in Italy, vaccination strategies in Spain, and the evaluation of influenza vaccines for older adults.

Collaboration
VIHTALI (Italian HTA projects); Evidera and CSL Seqirus (Spanish HTA project).
Research output
  • Fluad Tetra® Health Technology Assessment (2024)
  • Health Technology Assessment: a value-based tool for the evaluation of healthcare technologies. Reassessment of the cell-culture-derived quadrivalent influenza vaccine: Flucelvax Tetra® 2.0 (2022)
  • An Economic Evaluation of the Adjuvanted Quadrivalent Influenza Vaccine Compared with Standard-Dose Quadrivalent Influenza Vaccine in the Spanish Older Adult Population (2022)
  • Health Technology Assessment of the introduction of influenza vaccination for Italian children (2021)

Methotrexate and Adalimumab Immunogenicity in Axial Spondyloarthritis

Development and analysis of a mechanistic mathematical model investigating the effect of methotrexate on adalimumab immunogenicity in axial spondyloarthritis.

Collaboration
COST Action ENOTTA; Bulgarian Academy of Sciences; CHRU Tours and research collaborators
Research output
Methotrexate’s effect on cells and adalimumab immunogenicity in axial spondyloarthritis: A mathematical study (2026)
